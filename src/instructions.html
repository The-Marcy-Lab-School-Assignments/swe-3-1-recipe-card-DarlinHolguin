The Yaroa Dominican Street Food

Instructions

  1. deep fry or air fry fries as the base.
  2. Cook meat chicken(shred after cooking) or ground beef.
  3. Top ground beef or chicken over fries
  4. Sprinkle cheese over the steaming hot meat. If the cheese doesn't melt you can put it in the microwave for 90sec or until whenever the cheese melts.
  5. Drizzle with Mayo and ketchup, it's also popular practice to mix these sauces before adding known as MayoKetchup(additional instructions below), then serve right away.
  6. Instructions for MayoKetchup: Mix both sauces 3tbsp Mayonnaise & 2tbsp Ketchup. Then add a bit of garlic powder and mix these ingredient
